## Project pitch

**After90: understand the moment, inspect the evidence.**

After90 turns synthetic football events into contextual match intelligence for
casual fans, analysts and studio teams. Its deterministic engine computes scores,
statistics and important moments. A Microsoft Foundry-ready narrative planner
selects supported evidence and approved audience frames; the server renders
factual wording from those verified facts. Viewers can replay a moment, switch
between Fan and Analyst views, inspect supporting events, preview a compact
studio graphic and read an evidence-backed full-time story. A labelled template
fallback keeps the experience usable when a model is unavailable.

The problem is the gap between seeing a statistic and understanding its match
significance. After90 connects the same recorded facts to the right explanation
for the audience without pretending that shot counts prove tactics or chance
quality.
